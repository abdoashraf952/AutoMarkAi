# AutoMark AI — How We Work & Onboarding Process

## Our Approach

AutoMark AI does not believe in cookie-cutter marketing. Every client gets a custom strategy built around their specific business goals, target audience, and budget. We combine deep human strategy with AI-powered execution to deliver better results, faster.

Our process is built on four pillars:

1. **Diagnose** — understand where you are and where you want to go
2. **Strategise** — build a data-backed plan to get there
3. **Execute** — launch campaigns with speed and precision
4. **Optimise** — measure, learn, and improve continuously

---

## Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1 — Discovery Call (Free, 30 minutes)

We start with a free strategy call to understand your business:
- What are your current marketing channels and results?
- Who is your target customer?
- What are your revenue goals?
- What is your monthly marketing budget?
- Who are your main competitors?

This call has no obligation. At the end, we tell you honestly what we think will work and what won't.

### Step 2 — Proposal & Strategy

Within 48 hours of the discovery call, we send you a tailored proposal that includes:
- Recommended services and why
- Clear objectives and key performance indicators (KPIs)
- Timeline to first results
- Pricing breakdown

### Step 3 — Agreement & Payment

Once you approve the proposal:
- We send a service agreement for your signature (digital signature accepted)
- You pay the first month's retainer (or project deposit)
- You receive an onboarding form with everything we need to get started

### Step 4 — Onboarding (Days 1–5)

Our team sets everything up:
- Access requests for your ad accounts, social profiles, and website
- Facebook Pixel, Google Analytics 4, and conversion tracking installation
- Audience research and competitor analysis
- Creative brief preparation (ad visuals, copy, messaging)
- HubSpot CRM setup (if included in your package)

### Step 5 — Launch (Days 5–7)

- Campaigns go live
- Content calendar approved and posts scheduled
- You receive a welcome report with baseline metrics

### Step 6 — Ongoing Management & Reporting

Every week (or month, depending on package):
- We monitor performance daily and optimise in real time
- You receive a performance report with clear metrics, insights, and next steps
- Monthly strategy call to review results and plan the upcoming month
- Your account manager is reachable via WhatsApp for quick questions

## Communication & Reporting

### Your Account Manager
Every client has a dedicated account manager who owns your account and is your single point of contact. They coordinate between our creative, technical, and media buying teams on your behalf.

### Report Contents
Every performance report includes:
- Total ad spend and impressions
- Clicks and click-through rate (CTR)
- Leads generated and cost per lead
- Revenue and return on ad spend (ROAS) — for e-commerce
- Top-performing creatives and audiences
- What we changed this period and why
- Recommended actions for the next period

### Communication Channels
- WhatsApp: for quick questions and approvals (response within 2 hours during business hours)
- Email: for detailed briefs, reports, and formal requests
- Scheduled Zoom / Google Meet calls: weekly or monthly strategy sessions
- Project management tool (Notion or Trello): for content approvals and campaign tracking

## Frequently Asked Process Questions

**Can I approve content before it goes live?**
Yes. All social media content is shared with you for approval before posting. We use a shared Notion board or Google Drive folder where you can review and leave feedback.

**Can I make changes to the campaign after it launches?**
Yes, but frequent changes can disrupt campaign learning algorithms. We recommend reviewing performance for at least 7 days before making significant changes. We will advise you on timing.

**What if I want to pause or stop the campaign?**
You can pause campaigns at any time. Just let your account manager know and we will pause within 24 hours. Note that pausing resets the algorithm's learning phase, which can temporarily affect performance when you resume.

**Can I increase my budget mid-month?**
Yes. If a campaign is performing well and you want to scale, we can increase the budget at any time. Large increases (more than 20%) should be done gradually to avoid disrupting the algorithm.

**What happens at the end of the minimum contract term?**
Your contract auto-renews monthly. You can cancel with 30 days notice after the minimum term ends. We will always remind you before renewal.
