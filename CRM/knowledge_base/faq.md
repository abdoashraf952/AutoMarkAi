We work in both Arabic and English. Our team can deliver campaigns, content, and reports in either or both languages.

**How long has AutoMark AI been operating?**
AutoMark AI has been providing digital marketing services since 2020. We have worked with hundreds of businesses across MENA and have managed over $5 million in ad spend.

**Are you a certified agency?**
Yes. AutoMark AI is a certified Meta Business Partner and a certified Google Partner. Our team holds certifications in Google Ads, Meta Blueprint, HubSpot, and other major platforms.

## Pricing Questions

**How much do your services cost?**
Our pricing ranges widely depending on the service and package:
- Social media management: from $500/month
- Paid ads management: from $600/month (ad spend is additional)
- SEO: from $400/month
- Website landing page: from $500 (one-time)

- Bundle packages start at $950/month and save you up to 15%

Full pricing details are available in our pricing guide. Contact us for a custom quote tailored to your budget and goals.

**Is the ad spend budget included in the management fee?**
No. The ad spend budget (the money that goes to Meta, Google, TikTok, etc.) is paid directly to the platforms by you. Our fees cover strategy, campaign setup, creative production, optimisation, and reporting. We recommend at least $500/month in ad spend for Meta Ads campaigns to see meaningful results.

**Do you offer a free trial?**
We do not offer a free trial, but we do offer a free 30-minute discovery and strategy call where we analyse your current marketing and show you exactly how we would approach your growth. There is no obligation to sign up.

**What is the minimum contract length?**
Our retainer services require a minimum 3-month commitment. This is because digital marketing — especially SEO and social media — takes time to build momentum and deliver results. One-time projects (website builds, audits) have no minimum commitment.

**What payment methods do you accept?**
We accept bank transfer, credit card, PayPal, and Wise (international transfers). Monthly retainers are billed in advance on the 1st of each month.

**Can I cancel my contract?**
After the minimum 3-month term, you can cancel with 30 days written notice. We do not lock clients into long contracts — our goal is to keep you because of the results we deliver.
