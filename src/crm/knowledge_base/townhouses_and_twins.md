# AutoMark Real Estate — Townhouses, Twin Houses & Duplexes

## What Is the Difference?

| Type | Description | Typical Price Premium vs Apartment |
|------|-------------|-----------------------------------|
| **Townhouse** | Multi-floor home sharing walls on both sides with neighbours. Has private garden and rooftop. | +40–70% |
| **Twin House** | Two homes sharing one wall (mirror image). Each has private garden and rooftop. More privacy than townhouse. | +60–100% |
| **Duplex Apartment** | Two-floor apartment within a building. Does NOT have a private garden. | +20–40% |
| **Triplex / Sky Villa** | Three-floor apartment — often the top floors of a building with private rooftop terrace. | +50–80% |

## Duplex & Triplex Apartments

### What Makes a Duplex/Triplex Different from a Townhouse?
- A **duplex** is inside a building — no private garden (usually)
- A **townhouse** is a standalone multi-floor unit with its own garden and entrance
- Ground-floor duplexes ("garden duplexes") may include a private garden

## Why Choose a Townhouse Over an Apartment?

✅ **More space** — typically 2–3 floors with 200–400 m² built-up area
✅ **Private outdoor space** — garden, roof terrace, or both
✅ **Better sound insulation** — no neighbours above or below (townhouse/twin)
✅ **Higher resale value** — townhouses appreciate faster than apartments
✅ **Rental income** — townhouses attract higher rental rates from expat families
✅ **Feel of a villa** at a fraction of the price

## Why Choose an Apartment Over a Townhouse?

✅ **Lower maintenance** — no garden or roof to maintain
✅ **Lower price point** — apartment budgets start much lower
✅ **Building security** — concierge, doorman, building management
✅ **Investment liquidity** — apartments sell faster in the resale market
✅ **Easier to rent** — faster to find tenants for apartments
