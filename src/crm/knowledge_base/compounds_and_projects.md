# AutoMark Real Estate — Compounds & Master-Planned Communities

## What Is a Compound?

A compound (كمباوند) in Egypt is a gated, master-planned residential community that offers a mix of property types (villas, apartments, townhouses) within a shared infrastructure of amenities — pools, clubs, parks, schools, shops — all maintained by a professional facility management company.

Living in a compound provides security, community, higher property values, and a better quality of life compared to standalone urban apartments.

## Compound Amenities Checklist — What to Ask

When evaluating a compound, always ask:
- ✅ Is the compound gated 24/7 with CCTV?
- ✅ What is the annual maintenance fee (per m²)?
- ✅ Is there an on-site school? What curriculum?
- ✅ Is the clubhouse membership included or extra?
- ✅ What is the current delivery status?
- ✅ Is the title deed (طوابو) registered or still pending?
- ✅ What are the rules for home renovation and exterior changes?
- ✅ Is there a residents' WhatsApp community or management portal?
