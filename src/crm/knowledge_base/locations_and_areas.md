## 1. New Cairo (القاهرة الجديدة)

**Best for:** Families, professionals, premium lifestyle buyers
**Tagline:** Cairo's most prestigious residential destination

### Overview
New Cairo is Egypt's most established premium real estate market. Built in the 1990s–2000s as a planned extension east of Cairo, it has grown into a self-contained city with international schools, hospitals, malls, and restaurants — all within gated compound communities.

### Key Districts
- **5th Settlement (التجمع الخامس):** The most popular district. Home to Hyde Park, Mivida, Palm Hills, SODIC East. Access via Al Teseen Road and the Ring Road.
- **1st Settlement:** Established area, older compounds, lower prices, ready title deeds
- **3rd Settlement:** Mix of apartments and villas, more affordable than 5th Settlement
- **South Investors Area:** Newest district, ultra-luxury projects by Ora, Mountain View

### What's Here
- American University in Cairo (AUC)
- Cairo International Airport — 20 min drive
- Mall of Arabia, Point 90, CityStars Mall (30 min), Festival City
- SODIC Club, Palm Hills Club, Hyde Park Central Park
- International schools: NILES, Maadi British School, Futures Language Schools
- Hospitals: Dar Al-Fouad, As-Salam International, Katameya Health

### Distance from Key Points
- Downtown Cairo: 25–35 km (Ring Road — 30–45 min)
- Cairo Airport: 18 km (20–25 min)
- New Administrative Capital: 40 km (30–40 min)
- Suez Road: 15 km

### Price Range (2024–2025)
- Apartments: EGP 3,000,000 – EGP 20,000,000
- Townhouses: EGP 7,000,000 – EGP 18,000,000
- Villas: EGP 12,000,000 – EGP 55,000,000

## 2. Sheikh Zayed City (مدينة الشيخ زايد)

**Best for:** West Cairo professionals, families who work in Giza or October
**Tagline:** Cairo's premium west-side residential hub

### Overview
Sheikh Zayed is the premium residential district on the western edge of Greater Cairo, developed in the 1990s. Home to SODIC's flagship developments, the Allegria Golf Club, and the Westown retail destination.

### Key Areas
- **Beverly Hills, Sheikh Zayed:** SODIC's Allegria, Eastown, The Polygon
- **Hadayek Al Sheikh Zayed:** More affordable, mix of apartments and villas
- **Al Nozha and Rabwa:** Established family communities, good schools

### What's Here
- Westown Hub (retail, restaurants, offices)
- SODIC Club
- Allegria Golf Club (18-hole championship course)
- NILES British School, Misr Language School
- Dar Al-Fouad Hospital, Cleopatra Hospital
- Easy Ring Road and 26th July Corridor access

### Price Range (2024–2025)
- Apartments: EGP 3,500,000 – EGP 15,000,000
- Townhouses: EGP 6,500,000 – EGP 20,000,000
- Villas: EGP 10,000,000 – EGP 55,000,000

## 3. 6th of October City (مدينة السادس من أكتوبر)

**Best for:** Budget-friendly buyers, university families, west Cairo market
**Tagline:** Cairo's most affordable premium address

### Overview
6th of October is a large planned city 30 km west of Cairo, built in the 1970s. It hosts major universities, industrial zones, and a growing residential market. More affordable than Sheikh Zayed with good infrastructure.

### Key Neighbourhoods
- **First District to Seventh District:** Government-planned residential
- **Al Motamayez District:** Premium compounds (Better Home, etc.)
- **Sphinx Area:** Near Mall of Arabia, Juhayna Square

### What's Here
- Mall of Arabia (Egypt's largest mall)
- MSA University, 6th of October University, Misr International University (branch)
- Many hospitals including Al Zohour Specialized Hospital
- Easy access to Cairo-Alexandria Desert Road

### Price Range (2024–2025)
- Apartments: EGP 950,000 – EGP 6,000,000
- Townhouses: EGP 4,000,000 – EGP 12,000,000
- Villas: EGP 8,000,000 – EGP 25,000,000

## 4. New Administrative Capital (العاصمة الإدارية الجديدة)

**Best for:** Investors, government employees, future-forward buyers
**Tagline:** Egypt's boldest urban project — the city of tomorrow

### Overview
The New Administrative Capital (NAC) is Egypt's flagship mega-project — a brand-new capital city being built from scratch 45 km east of Cairo. All government ministries, Parliament, the Presidential Palace, and the new Central Bank are relocating here. With over 1 million residents expected by 2030, it is the highest-potential investment market in Egypt today.

### Key Districts for Residential
- **R7 District:** Premium residential — Il Bosco, Mountain View, Sarai extension, La Verde
- **R8 District:** Mixed use, near the CBD
- **R5 District:** More affordable entry points

### What's Completed / Coming Soon
- The Iconic Tower (Africa's tallest, 79 floors) — COMPLETED
- Grand Mosque of Egypt (second largest mosque in the world) — COMPLETED
- New Parliament building — COMPLETED
- Government ministries (most relocated) — COMPLETED
- Opera House, cultural district — under construction
- New Capital Airport — under construction (opening 2025)
- Monorail connecting NAC to Cairo — operational on some segments

### Investment Rationale
- Prices are still 30–40% lower than equivalent New Cairo properties
- Once government relocation is complete, demand will surge
- Off-plan purchases today are expected to double within 5 years
- Rental demand is growing fast (government employees, diplomats)

### Price Range (2024–2025)
- Apartments: EGP 2,200,000 – EGP 12,000,000
- Townhouses: EGP 5,500,000 – EGP 16,000,000
- Villas: EGP 10,000,000 – EGP 30,000,000

## 5. North Coast & Sahel (الساحل الشمالي)

**Best for:** Summer holiday buyers, high-income families, investment (rental)
**Tagline:** Egypt's Mediterranean Riviera

### Overview
The North Coast (Sahel) stretches 250 km along the Mediterranean from Alexandria to Marsa Matrouh. It is Egypt's most exclusive summer destination, with crystal-clear turquoise water, white sand beaches, and world-class resorts. Properties here are primarily summer residences used June–September, with strong rental income potential.

### Key Areas (West to East from Alexandria)

| Area | Distance from Cairo | Prestige Level | Price Level |
|------|--------------------|-----------------|----|
| Agami / Hannoville | 230 km | Medium | Affordable |
| Sidi Krier (Km 100) | 290 km | Medium-High | Mid |
| Marassi (Km 125) | 315 km | Very High | Luxury |
| Sidi Abdelrahman (Km 200) | 390 km | Ultra Premium | Luxury–Ultra |
| Fouka Bay (Km 115) | 305 km | Very High | Luxury |

### Top Resorts — North Coast
1. **Marassi (Emaar)** — Km 125, Mediterranean village design, private beach, marina
2. **Hacienda Bay (Palm Hills)** — Km 200, 5 km private beach, golf course
3. **Fouka Bay (Tatweer Misr)** — Km 115, Crystal Lagoon, modern design
4. **Hacienda Waters** — Km 190, ultra-luxury, limited units
5. **Bo Island (Emaar)** — Km 63, newer development, competitive pricing
6. **Amwaj Ain Sokhna** — Red Sea (different from North Coast)

### What to Expect
- Peak season: June 15 – September 15 (school holidays)
- Off-season: Almost empty — managed security maintains properties
- Summer rental yield: EGP 30,000–300,000/month depending on unit size and resort
- Some resorts offer managed rental programs (developer rents your unit when you're not there)

### North Coast Price Range (2024–2025)
- Studio/Chalet (1 BR): EGP 2,500,000 – EGP 5,000,000
- 2 BR Chalet: EGP 4,000,000 – EGP 8,000,000
- 3 BR Villa/Chalet: EGP 8,000,000 – EGP 20,000,000
- Beach Standalone Villa: EGP 20,000,000 – EGP 80,000,000+

## 6. Ain Sokhna (عين سخنة) — Red Sea

**Best for:** Weekend getaway buyers from Cairo
**Tagline:** Cairo's weekend escape, 1 hour away

### Overview
Ain Sokhna on the Red Sea coast is the closest beach destination to Cairo (130 km, 1.5 hours). Popular for weekend trips year-round. Unlike North Coast, the climate is pleasant almost all year.

### Key Projects
- **Porto Sokhna (Amer Group)** — iconic resort with rides, waterpark, private beach
- **Telal Sokhna (Tatweer Misr)** — modern chalets, Red Sea views
- **Mountain View Sokhna** — well-managed, good community
- **Swissôtel Resort Ain Sokhna** — hotel-branded residences

### Ain Sokhna Price Range
- Studio/1 BR Chalet: EGP 1,200,000 – EGP 3,000,000
- 2 BR Chalet: EGP 2,500,000 – EGP 5,500,000
- Villa: EGP 5,000,000 – EGP 18,000,000

## How to Choose Your Location

**Ask yourself:**
1. **Where do you work?** Choose within 30–45 minutes max commute
2. **Do you have children?** Prioritise areas with international schools
3. **Is this investment or personal use?** NAC and North Coast for investment; New Cairo for lifestyle
4. **What is your budget?** NAC and October offer the best price per m²
5. **Do you want a summer property?** North Coast or Ain Sokhna
6. **Do you need it now or can you wait?** Ready units in New Cairo vs off-plan deals in NAC
