## Property Types

**Q: What is the difference between a villa and a townhouse?**
A: A standalone villa is fully detached — it shares no walls with neighbours and sits on its own plot of land. A townhouse shares walls on both sides with neighbouring units (like a row house) but has its own private entrance, garden, and rooftop. Twin houses share one wall with one neighbour only. Villas offer maximum privacy; townhouses are typically 40–60% cheaper.

**Q: What does "compound" mean?**
A: A compound (كمباوند) is a gated, master-planned residential community with shared amenities — swimming pools, gyms, parks, playgrounds, restaurants, and sometimes schools — all managed by a facilities company. All our villa, townhouse, and apartment listings are within secure, amenity-rich compounds.

**Q: What is the difference between "core & shell," "semi-finished," and "fully finished"?**
A: 
- **Core & shell:** Bare concrete — you do all finishing work yourself (flooring, kitchen, bathrooms, paintwork). Budget EGP 3,000–5,000/m² for finishing.
- **Semi-finished:** Plastered walls, basic electrical and plumbing. You add flooring, kitchen, bathrooms. Budget EGP 1,500–2,500/m² for finishing.
- **Fully finished:** Move-in ready with flooring, kitchen cabinets, bathrooms, and sometimes appliances and ACs. No additional work needed.

**Q: What is a garden apartment?**
A: A ground-floor apartment that comes with an exclusive private garden — typically 50–300 m². Garden apartments cost more than standard upper-floor units but offer private outdoor space similar to a townhouse.

**Q: What is a penthouse?**
A: The top floor (or top two floors) of a residential building, usually with a large private rooftop terrace. Penthouses command a 30–80% premium over standard floor apartments and offer panoramic views and the highest level of privacy within a building.

## Buying Process

**Q: How do I reserve a unit?**
A: The process is:
1. Choose your unit and confirm the price and payment plan
2. Pay a booking fee (رسوم حجز) — usually EGP 25,000–100,000 — to hold the unit for 7–14 days
3. Sign the Sale and Purchase Agreement (SPA / عقد بيع ابتدائي) within 14 days
4. Pay the down payment at contract signing
5. Begin instalment schedule as per contract

**Q: How long does it take from reservation to delivery?**
A: For under-construction units, delivery typically takes 2–4 years. For ready units, handover can happen within 30–90 days. We always confirm the expected delivery date before you book.

**Q: Do I get a title deed (طوابو)?**
A: For most new developer projects, the title deed is issued 1–3 years after final payment and delivery, once the developer registers the community with the Real Estate Registry. In the meantime, you hold a Sale and Purchase Agreement (SPA) which is legally binding. For resale properties from individual owners, insist on a registered title deed before buying.

**Q: Can I customise my unit (change layouts, finishes)?**
A: This depends on the developer and the delivery stage. Some developers allow customisation if requested early in construction (e.g., merging two rooms, changing kitchen position). Fully finished units generally do not allow changes. We negotiate on your behalf for any customisation requests.

**Q: What documents do I need to buy a property?**
A: For Egyptian nationals:
- National ID (الرقم القومي)
- Down payment (cash or bank transfer)

For foreign nationals:
- Valid passport
- Bank transfer proof in foreign currency (USD, EUR, etc.)
- Entry stamp to Egypt

## Payment & Financing

**Q: What is the minimum down payment required?**
A: The lowest available down payments are 5% (MNHD Sarai, Better Home, Tatweer Misr BLOOMFIELDS). Most premium developers require 10–20%. On a EGP 5,000,000 unit, a 10% down payment means EGP 500,000 upfront.

**Q: Are developer payment plans interest-free?**
A: Yes — all our developers offer 0% interest instalment plans. The total price does not increase whether you pay cash or over 10 years. This is standard in the Egyptian market.

**Q: Can I take out a bank mortgage?**
A: Yes, but only on ready units with a registered title deed. Banks offer mortgages up to 20 years at 12–15% annual interest. For new launches, developer instalment plans are almost always a better deal (0% interest vs 14% bank rate).

**Q: What if I want to sell before the unit is delivered?**
A: You can sell your unit "on plan" — this means selling your purchase contract to a new buyer. The new buyer pays you your equity (what you've paid plus any appreciation) and takes over the remaining instalments. The developer charges a transfer fee of 2–5%. This is common and legal.

**Q: Can I miss an instalment?**
A: Most contracts allow 30–60 days grace period. After that, late fees apply (typically 1–2% per month on overdue amount). Persistent default may result in contract cancellation. In that case, most developer contracts refund paid amounts minus a 10–15% cancellation fee. Always contact your developer proactively if you foresee payment difficulties.

## Investment Questions

**Q: Is Egyptian real estate a good investment?**
A: Yes — Egyptian real estate has historically been one of the best inflation hedges and stores of value in the country. Key reasons:
- EGP has depreciated significantly vs USD — property values in EGP terms have increased 3–5x in 5 years
- Strong rental demand in New Cairo, Sheikh Zayed, and NAC
- Population growth driving housing demand
- Government mega-projects (NAC, North Coast development) boosting values in targeted areas

**Q: What is the expected rental yield?**
A: Gross rental yields range from 8–14% annually in key markets:
- New Cairo apartments: 8–10%
- NAC apartments: 10–14% (high demand from government relocations)
- North Coast (summer seasonal): 10–15%
- Sheikh Zayed: 8–10%

**Q: Which area has the best investment return right now?**
A: The New Administrative Capital (NAC) offers the best combination of price appreciation potential and rental yield. Prices are still 30–40% lower than equivalent New Cairo properties, but demand is growing rapidly as ministries relocate. Our investment team can model ROI for any specific property.

**Q: Can I rent out my property?**
A: Yes — there are no restrictions on renting residential properties in Egypt. Short-term summer rental (Airbnb-style) is popular for North Coast and Ain Sokhna properties. Long-term annual leases are common in New Cairo and NAC. Some compounds allow only long-term leases (check compound rules before buying for rental purposes).
