## Apartment Buying Guide

### What to Check Before Buying
1. **Title Deed (طوابو)** — ask if the unit has a registered title deed or only a preliminary sale contract
2. **Finishing level** — core & shell / semi-finished / fully finished — costs EGP 2,500–4,500 per m² to finish
3. **Service charges** — typically EGP 100–300 per m² per year
4. **Parking** — included or separate purchase? How many spaces?
5. **Floor and view** — upper floors cost 10–20% more; garden apartments may cost the same but offer outdoor space
6. **Delivery date** — request it in the contract with penalty clauses for delay

### Financing Options
- **Developer payment plan:** 5–20% down, 4–10 years (most common)
- **Bank mortgage:** Banque Misr, CIB, NBE, ADIB — up to 20 years, 12–14% interest
- **Cash discount:** typically 5–10% off list price for full payment
