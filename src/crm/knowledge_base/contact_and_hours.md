## Viewing Appointments

### How to Book a Viewing
1. Tell us which compound or property you are interested in
2. Confirm your preferred date and time
3. We arrange the viewing with the developer's on-site sales team
4. Our agent accompanies you to give you independent advice

### Viewing Locations
- **New Cairo compounds:** We have viewing availability 7 days a week
- **Sheikh Zayed / 6th October:** Sunday–Friday, morning and afternoon
- **New Administrative Capital:** Available 7 days — 45-minute drive from New Cairo
- **North Coast:** Viewings arranged for weekends — we travel with you
- **Ain Sokhna:** Weekend trips arranged on request

### Virtual Viewings (for overseas buyers)
- Live video call walkthrough via WhatsApp or Zoom
- 360° virtual tour videos for most properties
- Drone footage and compound overview videos available on request
