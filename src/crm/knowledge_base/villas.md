## Key Villa Buying Tips

1. **Delivery date** — always confirm in the contract. Under-construction villas carry more risk but offer better prices.
2. **Finishing spec** — core & shell (unfinished), semi-finished, or fully finished. Ask what is included.
3. **Compound amenities** — clubhouse membership fees typically EGP 5,000–25,000/year on top of purchase.
4. **Re-sale vs developer** — developer prices are typically 20–30% lower but require longer wait times.
5. **Maintenance fees** — budget EGP 150–400 per m² per year for compound maintenance.
